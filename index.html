How I work

先把问题想清楚，再让工具发挥作用。Clarify the problem before putting tools to work.

拆解需求Clarify

确认目标、受众、约束和验收标准，避免一开始就被工具带着走。Define the goal, audience, constraints, and success criteria before choosing tools.

组织协作Orchestrate

为 AI 智能体划分角色、建立审阅门，并保留清晰的任务交接记录。Assign roles to AI agents, add review gates, and keep handoffs traceable.

交付与复盘Deliver

产出可查看的结果，验证链接与内容，再把有效方法沉淀为可复用流程。Ship a reviewable result, verify it, and capture the method as a reusable workflow.
